dakuwon-rclone

A private Google Drive transfer tool, operated by Saiyan Corp.

dakuwon-rclone runs rclone, the open-source file sync program, on its operator's own computer. It copies large research files, such as model checkpoints and datasets, between that computer and the operator's Google Drive, and checks that each copy arrived intact.

What it does with Google Drive

  • Uploads files from the operator's computer to their Google Drive.
  • Downloads files from Google Drive back to that computer.
  • Lists, moves, renames, or deletes Drive files only when the operator tells it to.
  • Compares checksums to confirm copies are correct, and reports storage used and free.

Who can use it

The app is for its operator's own use and is not offered to the public. It has no server or database of its own: sign-in tokens stay in a local file on the operator's computer. See the privacy policy for details.
